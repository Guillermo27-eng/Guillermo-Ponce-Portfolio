# Urban Resilience Index: Baldwin Hills & Urban Watersheds Conservancy

*USC Public Exchange · Project Analyst, GIS & Climate Resilience*

## The problem
The Baldwin Hills & Urban Watersheds Conservancy had grown from 2 to 70 square miles and gained access to $700M in California Proposition 4 climate funding. It needed a better way to know where that money should go first.

## What I did
I evaluated the Urban Resilience Index (URI), a GIS tool built to find high-vulnerability communities. For each indicator in its five categories (Community Empowerment, Water Management, Biodiversity, Extreme Heat & Fire, and Park Access), I scored accessibility, transparency, and comprehensiveness. I also compared the URI against three outside tools: the Nature Conservancy's Resilient Land Mapping Tool, the Connecticut Environmental Justice Mapping Tool, and the Living Infrastructure Field Kit.

I looked closely at environmental burden indicators such as pollution exposure, hazardous facilities, impervious surfaces, and public health sensitivity, and at how each one helps or hides the communities most at risk.

## What came out of it
Four sets of recommendations to improve the tool: data improvements, interface enhancements, user accessibility, and future development. They tie the URI to Prop 4 investment and infrastructure planning, so decisions about where to spend reach the highest-vulnerability neighborhoods of South Los Angeles.
