# Lee Andrews Group

*Strategic Communications Intern · Los Angeles, California*

Lee Andrews Group is a public affairs and strategic communications firm that supports infrastructure, transportation, and community engagement work across Los Angeles County.

## The problem
The firm was managing 50+ RFP proposals for multiple LA County clients with no central system. Work lived in emails, scattered documents, and individual memory.

## What I did
| Stage | Tool | What it did |
|---|---|---|
| Organize | Airtable | Built a centralized project tracker and stakeholder tracking systems to standardize workflows |
| Produce | Google Gemini | Sped up coordination and drafting across 50+ client RFP proposals |
| Report | Google Analytics, Excel | Turned LA Metro program performance into weekly reports for Metro Joint Development and CVI programs |

I also supported community engagement projects tied to infrastructure and transportation, and contributed to communications and reporting for Metro and other public sector initiatives.

## Result
A 90% improvement in operational efficiency through automated tracking and real-time visibility across client portfolios.

## Tools
Airtable · Google Analytics · Google Gemini · Excel · Microsoft Teams · workflow automation
